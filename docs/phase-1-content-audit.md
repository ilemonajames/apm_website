# APM Phase 1 Content Audit

Status values: **Retain**, **Verify**, **Create**, **Archive**.

| Content area | Current source | Status | Action required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Party name, logo and motto | Existing APM materials | Retain | Obtain original vector logo and written usage approval. |
| Mission, vision and values | Existing APM website and constitution | Verify | Approve final wording for public pages. |
| Policy priorities | Existing manifesto summaries | Verify | Check every summary against the authoritative manifesto. |
| Constitution | Existing APM PDF | Verify | Confirm current approved version, date and file. |
| Manifesto | Proposed 2026 PDF link | Verify | Confirm URL, publication date and authority. |
| National leadership | INEC listing and existing APM website | Verify | Confirm names, roles, tenure, biographies and portraits. |
| State and LGA chapters | Not supplied | Create | Provide approved office directory and public contacts. |
| News and press releases | Existing site contains sample content | Archive | Do not migrate template finance stories; supply approved newsroom content. |
| Events | Not supplied | Create | Define event owner, approval process and launch content. |
| Membership registration | Existing external portal | Retain temporarily | Confirm required fields, approval rules and migration approach. |
| Polling-agent information | Requirements supplied | Create | Approve eligibility, review, allocation and capacity rules. |
| Secretariat address and phones | INEC listing | Verify | Confirm current public contact details. |
| Privacy, retention and deletion | Not supplied | Create | Obtain legal and operational approval before collecting member data. |
| Approved photography | Not supplied | Create | Collect portraits, events and secretariat images with captions and permissions. |

## Immediate approval requests

1. Original APM logo files and brand owner approval.
2. Authoritative manifesto and constitution files.
3. Current national leadership register with tenure details.
4. Approved state and LGA chapter directory.
5. Official email addresses, phone numbers and social accounts.
6. Membership verification and approval rules.
7. Polling-agent eligibility, reviewer hierarchy and allocation rules.
8. Privacy, retention, deletion and authorised export policy.
9. Approved photographs and documented permission to publish.

## Content excluded from migration

- Generic finance and investment articles present in the old website template.
- Unverified membership statistics or election countdowns.
- Duplicate leadership entries and sample personnel profiles.
- Generic photographs presented as evidence of APM activity.
