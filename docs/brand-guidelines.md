# APM Digital Brand Foundation

## Identity

- Full name: Allied Peoples' Movement.
- Short name: APM.
- Motto: Nigeria First.
- Values: Integrity, Sacrifice and Service.

## Core colours

| Name | Value | Primary use |
|---|---|---|
| APM Green | `#087541` | Primary actions, headings and identity areas |
| Deep Green | `#063f2b` | Footer, leadership bands and high-contrast surfaces |
| APM Blue | `#1767a8` | Secondary emphasis and participation areas |
| Ink | `#10241b` | Main text |
| White | `#ffffff` | Primary background and reversed text |
| Paper | `#f5f7f3` | Quiet section background |
| Focus Yellow | `#f2c94c` | Keyboard focus and limited emphasis |

## Usage rules

- Use only approved logo artwork; never redraw, stretch or recolour the emblem.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least one quarter of its displayed width.
- Use green for primary actions and blue for secondary emphasis.
- Keep body text at 16px or larger and maintain visible keyboard focus.
- Use authentic, captioned APM photographs with documented permission.
- Do not use generic political imagery as evidence of party events.
- Publish leadership names, titles and portraits only after verification.
